Privacy Policy

Sai Ireland app · Sathya Sai Organisation Ireland · Last updated 2 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Sai Ireland mobile app (package name com.shayshank.saiireland), operated by the Sathya Sai Organisation Ireland, collects, uses, and protects your information. The app is operated from Ireland and this policy is written to comply with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

1. Information we collect

CategoryWhat we collectWhy
Account information Name, email address, and profile photo from Google Sign-In To create your account and personalise the app
Phone number Optional, entered by you Used only if you choose to provide it (e.g. seva sign-up)
Messages to admin Subject and message text you submit via "Contact Admin" To respond to your enquiry or prayer request
Poll votes & event RSVPs Your selection, linked to your account To enforce one vote per person and show live results
Push notification token Firebase Cloud Messaging device token To deliver event, poll, and announcement notifications you've opted into
Crash & analytics data Device model, OS version, app usage events, crash logs Only if you opt in — helps us fix bugs and improve the app. No personal content is included.

2. How we use your information

  • To operate core features: events, polls, bhajans, announcements, and the WhatsApp join request flow.
  • To let admins respond to messages you send via "Contact Admin".
  • To send push notifications for categories you've enabled in Settings.
  • To diagnose crashes and improve reliability, only if you've enabled analytics/crash reporting.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Third-party services

The app relies on the following Google / Firebase services to operate. Data processed by these services is subject to Google's Privacy Policy:

  • Firebase Authentication — Google Sign-In
  • Cloud Firestore — stores your profile, events, polls, messages
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging — push notifications
  • Firebase Analytics — optional, opt-in usage analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics — optional, opt-in crash reporting

4. Your choices & controls

  • Analytics and crash reporting are off by default — you choose to enable them during sign-up consent, and can change this anytime in Settings.
  • You can toggle individual push notification categories in Settings.
  • You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete Account. This removes your profile information. Poll votes and messages are retained in anonymised form (not linked to your identity) so that poll results and community records stay accurate.

5. Data retention

Your account data is retained while your account is active. When you delete your account, your profile is anonymised immediately and your authentication credentials are removed. Anonymised poll votes and messages may be retained for community record-keeping purposes.

6. Your GDPR rights

If you are in the EU/EEA (including Ireland), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request erasure of your data (see "Delete Account" above)
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Data portability

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Children's privacy

The app is intended for members of the Sathya Sai Organisation Ireland community and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date above.
